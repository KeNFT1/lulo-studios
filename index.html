How we build

Small studio.
Hard edges.

Start with the real workflow.

Product decisions come from the job someone is trying to finish, not a feature checklist.

Make trust visible.

Sources, receipts, privacy boundaries, audit history, and honest status labels belong in the product.

Use AI where it earns its keep.

Agents handle research and repetitive judgment while deterministic systems guard critical boundaries.

Ship, measure, revise.

A working release creates better questions than a polished concept ever will.
